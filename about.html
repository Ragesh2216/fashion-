Our Heritage

Two Decades of Building fashion Excellence

From a single research lab in Boston to a global network of biofashion engineers, Stackly has grown by solving the problems that matter most to clients and designers alike.

Our journey began with a commitment to innovation, precision, and compassion — values that continue to guide every decision we make.

Our Journey

A Legacy of Trust and Innovation

Founded with a vision to revolutionize fashion procurement, Stackly started as a small team of engineers and fashion store consultants. Today, we are a global leader in providing high-precision fashion equipment.

We understand that in fashion store, every second and every millimeter counts. That's why we meticulously vet every manufacturer and every component that enters our catalog.
